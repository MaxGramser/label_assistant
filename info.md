# Label Assistant

A Canva-style label designer for Home Assistant. Design labels with text in
playful fonts, shapes, emoji stickers, photos, QR codes and frames on a canvas
that is exactly your DYMO 99014 label (54 × 101 mm) — then print with one tap
via the [Label Printer add-on](https://github.com/MaxGramser/fridge_assistant/tree/main/addon/label_printer)
that ships with Fridge Assistant (required for printing, not for designing).
Saves reusable templates, shows a true black & white print preview, and is
friendly enough for kids.
